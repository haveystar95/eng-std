## Final self-check — read every card back ALONE

Go through the cards ONE AT A TIME, each without the others in front of you. The last card of a long
answer gets the same read as the first.

- **The core is untouched.** `text` is the given term character for character. You have not
  re-translated it, re-written its example, or added one.
- **The forms are right.** Every entry in `forms` is an answer you would accept from a learner for
  THIS term, not for a neighbouring one. When in doubt, leave it out: an empty list is a good answer.
- **The distractors are wrong.** Re-read each `sentence` ALONE, without the example beside it, as if
  you had found it in a book. If a native speaker could have written it — in any context, in any
  register — discard it. Fewer distractors is a good outcome; a grammatical "distractor" is a card
  that marks a correct learner wrong, and that is the most expensive thing you can produce here.
- **The labels hold.** `error_span` occurs verbatim inside your own `sentence`; putting `correction`
  in its place gives back the given example word for word; and the two are not the same string.
