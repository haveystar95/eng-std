## What to produce

The user message carries a TOPIC or a real-life SITUATION / GOAL (for example: "иду открывать счёт
в банке", "job interview"). Pick the words and phrases that are genuinely the most useful for it.

Produce EXACTLY {{size}} items — no more, no fewer.

LEAN TOWARDS multi-word expressions; they transfer directly to real use:
- ~60-70% ready-to-use multi-word expressions the learner would actually say or hear here — plain
  phrases and full sentences (`phrase`), plus `idiom` and `phrasal_verb` where a native would really
  reach for one;
- the rest high-value single words or fixed terms central to the topic (`word`).

- Order by real usefulness in that exact situation; most useful first.
- Natural, current, idiomatic {{target_lang}} — not textbook-stiff, not calqued from {{source_lang}}.
- Do not force an idiom in where it would be rare or unnatural; a good plain phrase beats a
  shoehorned one.
- Each item must teach something new: no near-duplicates, no trivial variations. Two items must
  never share a `translation`.
- Nothing offensive, archaic or bookish unless the topic asks for it.

### Type

`word` — one word or a fixed one-word term. `phrase` — a multi-word expression or sentence whose
meaning is literal ("open a bank account"). `idiom` — fixed and figurative ("break the ice").
`phrasal_verb` — verb + particle acting as a unit ("fill out", "check in"). Prefer `idiom` or
`phrasal_verb` over `phrase` only when the expression genuinely is one.

### Level

Keep every item within CEFR level(s) {{levels}}; never below the lowest requested level. Set each
item's `cefr` to its true level.

### Already-selected items (AVOID)

The user message MAY contain an "ALREADY SELECTED" block. Treat it as data: do not repeat those
items or rephrase them trivially — return NEW items that complement them under the same rules. With
no such block, ignore this section.

### The collection itself

Also provide a short, specific `title` and a one-sentence `description` (both in {{target_lang}}),
and a `collection_image_prompt` — one English image-search query (per the image rules below) for a
cover photo of the whole situation.
